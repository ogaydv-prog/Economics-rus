Course Syllabus

SEE2116 Economics | Software Engineering (3 ECTS)

Course Overview & Details

Course Code & Name: SEI2116 Economics

Program: Software Engineering (SE-2401/2501)

Credits & Hours: 3 ECTS (Total: 90 hours | 15 Classroom hours + 15 Online hours + 60 Independent work hours)

Instructor: Dmitriy Ogay, MBA, Professor of the Practice (d.ogay@almau.edu.kz)

Description: The course introduces students to key concepts of micro- and macroeconomics necessary for understanding the modern tech-driven business environment. Special attention is paid to markets, consumer behavior, economic policy, and digital industry applications.

Expected Learning Outcomes

  • LO 1: Understand national economic measurement methodologies and rating indicators.
  • LO 2: Explain trends in consumer and firm behavior, demand factors, and market equilibrium.
  • LO 3: Provide economic assessments of current global market events and digital economics.
  • LO 4: Analyze macroeconomic fluctuations (inflation, unemployment) and government policies.
  • LO 5: Evaluate market imperfections and identify rationale for state intervention.
  • LO 6: Trace relationships between financial markets, tech industries, and national economy.

Academic Integrity & Artificial Intelligence Policy

Students must adhere strictly to AlmaU Code of Ethics. Independent execution of tasks is required.

Allowed AI Usage:

  • Calculations automation and economic data modeling.
  • Assisting in understanding complex microeconomic models.
  • Grammar refinement and research assistance.

Prohibited AI Usage: Replacing student critical thinking with automated text generation (e.g., ChatGPT submitting complete essays without original analysis) is considered academic misconduct.

Classroom & Exam Ethics

  • At least 70% attendance is mandatory to pass the course.
  • Punctuality and polite academic behavior in classroom and online sessions.
  • Submitting assignments on time via Platonus and official channels.
  • Lateness disrupts classes and leads to missed attendance marks.

Evaluation & Grading System

Final Grade Calculation:

Final Grade = (Rating RK1 + RK2) / 2 × 0.6 + Final Exam × 0.4

  • Grade A (95-100%): 4.0 - Excellent comprehension and analytical skills.
  • Grade B (75-89%): 2.67-3.33 - Good understanding and solid case solutions.
  • Grade C (60-74%): 1.67-2.33 - Satisfactory knowledge without deep elaboration.
  • Grade F (0-24%): 0.0 - Fail.
